## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of computational rigor and editorial poise, articulated as "An Intelligence in Motion." Designed for an AI/ML engineer and researcher, the visual language avoids generic silicon cliches (such as hyper-saturated neons, generic wireframes, and sci-fi tropes) in favor of a cerebral, tactile, and restrained digital presence. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a hybrid of **Technical Editorial** and **Organic Brutalism**:
- **Target Audience:** Engineering peers, machine learning researchers, technical recruiters, and collaborators seeking depth, clarity, and structural sophistication.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires quiet authority, cognitive clarity, disciplined craftsmanship, and kinetic curiosity.
- **Design Philosophy:** Structural elements emulate hardware schematics, laboratory notebooks, and algorithmic telemetry. Spatial composition relies on calculated asymmetry, high-density metadata micro-annotations, razor-thin framing lines, and calibrated luminescence rather than heavy dropped shadows.

## Colors

The palette—Mineral Nocturne—draws inspiration from petrified geological strata and computational instrument panels. It leverages an ultra-deep, muted tonal spectrum to reduce visual fatigue while establishing dramatic contrast with bone-toned text and botanical technical signals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Obsidian Ink (`#171A19`):** Canvas bedrock. Absorptive, carbonaceous base layer serving as the primary void.
- **Graphite Mist (`#252B28`):** Elevated structural substrate. Used for containers, interactive panels, navigation docks, and card backgrounds.
- **Deep Moss (`#39483E`):** Structural perimeter and framing. Applied to precision dividers, cell borders, and inactive system states.
- **Porcelain (`#F0EEE6`):** High-readability optical foreground. Reserved for editorial display titles, active readouts, and primary content.
- **Ash Grey (`#8F9790`):** Secondary informational tier. Applied to editorial subtitles, secondary copy, inline labels, and tabular metadata.
- **Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0`):** Primary kinetic accent. Signifies active computational nodes, interactive focal points, dynamic progress vectors, and hovered states.
- **Oxidized Copper (`#C88763`):** Critical telemetry and secondary highlight. Reserved for alerts, specialized ML model training tags, high-priority research markers, and selective hover states.

### Contrast & Functional Mapping
- Primary background (`#171A19`) with porcelain text (`#F0EEE6`) achieves a contrast ratio well above 13:1, exceeding WCAG AAA standards.
- Secondary interactive elements utilize Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0`) against Graphite Mist (`#252B28`) with micro-borders of Deep Moss (`#39483E`) to maintain spatial structure without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a high-contrast triad between expressive headline geometry, neutral analytical narrative, and monospaced diagnostic telemetry.

### Font Roles
- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides mechanical yet idiosyncratic cadence. Its eccentric ink-traps and angular terminations invoke engineered intelligence. Reserved for project names, section titles, and macro statements.
- **Narrative & Body (Inter):** Neutral, hyper-legible, functional. Balances the personality of the display face by disappearing into long-form research documentation and project methodology prose.
- **Telemetry & Labels (Space Mono):** Operates as the systematic voice of the engineer. Used exclusively for micro-annotations, execution metrics, mathematical weights, timestamps, status chips, and code snippets.

### Rules of Engagement
- Apply uppercase styling to `annotation` and `label-md` tokens with loosened letter spacing (`0.04em`–`0.06em`).
- Numerical values within analytical cards must always render in monospaced type to preserve vertical decimal alignments.
- Large titles must employ negative kerning (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) to eliminate optical tracking bloat.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy rejects uniform symmetry in favor of **Computational Editorial Architecture**—a deliberate, asymmetric balance inspired by archival broadsheets and IDE interfaces.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `2.5rem` (`gutter-lg`) spacing and `3rem` (`margin`) outer canvas padding. Complex sections follow an offset 4/8 or 5/7 column split to prioritize contextual annotation alongside primary work.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (`gutter`) spacing and `2rem` (`margin-tablet`) margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column linear layout with `1rem` (`gutter-sm`) and `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`) margins.

### Rhythm & Alignment Rules
- **Micro-Annotations:** Technical indicators, dates, and category tags hover within structural margins or sit pinned to the top-left coordinate of modular cells.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections step down in proportional increments (`space-xl` to `space-3xl`), maintaining intentional void spaces that frame high-density information arrays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established through **Structural Tonal Stratification** and **Subsurface Luminescence**, explicitly omitting soft blurry drop shadows and blurry light sources.

### Depth Stratification
- **Ground Floor (`#171A19`):** Base canvas containing passive background grids, rule dividers, and non-interactive layout scaffolding.
- **Tier 1 — Plate Surface (`#252B28`):** Cards, terminal shells, modular lists, and navigation rails.
- **Tier 2 — Hover & Dynamic Plane (`#2B332F`):** Activated card states, expanded details, and interactive inputs.

### Outlines and Ghost Borders
- Elevation breaks are realized via crisp, 1px low-contrast structural rules using Deep Moss (`#39483E`).
- Active or focused modules shift their border state from `#39483E` to Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0` at 50% opacity), creating immediate structural distinction without dimension shifts.

### Ambient Technical Glow (Luminescence)
- To signify dynamic ML computation or focal execution, elements utilize a hyper-restrained ambient edge glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #A8B8A0, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(168, 184, 160, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural precision, calibration, and computational tension.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Curvature Index:** Strictly Level 1 (`roundedness: 1`). Structural containers, cards, buttons, and system controls adhere to `0.25rem` (4px) radii. Large structural sections use a maximum of `0.5rem` (8px). Rounding exists solely to prevent harsh pixel aliasing, never to appear soft or bubbly.
- **Data Cells & Badges:** Monospaced technical tags and status flags use sharp geometry (`0px` border radius) or minimal `2px` micro-chamfers to echo instrument gauges.
- **Framing Lines:** 1px hairline dividers separate asymmetric blocks, maintaining disciplined containment across all components.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Graphite Mist background (`#252B28`), 1px solid Sage Signal border (`#A8B8A0`), Porcelain text (`#F0EEE6`). On hover: background shifts to Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0`), text shifts to Obsidian Ink (`#171A19`), accompanied by a 150ms linear transition.
- **Technical Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px Deep Moss border (`#39483E`), Ash Grey text (`#8F9790`). Includes a prefixed micro-annotation symbol (`[>]` or `//`). On hover: border and text shift to Porcelain (`#F0EEE6`).
- **Telemetry Ghost:** Minimal text button in Space Mono with a micro-underline that expands dynamically from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Chips & Technical Tags
- **Model / Tooling Badges:** Sharp or 2px corner radius, Obsidian Ink surface (`#171A19`), 1px Deep Moss outline (`#39483E`). Text rendered in Space Mono 11px uppercase in Ash Grey (`#8F9790`).
- **Status Indicators:** Live computational signals utilize a 6px circular dot pulsating softly between Deep Moss (`#39483E`) and Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0`), paired with status text (e.g., `SYS: ACTIVE`, `STATUS: OPTIMIZED`).

### Lists & Modular Indexing
- Lists use tabular row lines (`border-bottom: 1px solid #39483E`).
- Each list entry spans across asymmetric grid columns: Project Index (e.g., `01 //`), Title in Space Grotesk, ML Domain / Tech Stack in Ash Grey, and an interactive directional glyph (`→`) that translates 4px rightward on hover.

### Cards & Modular Research Units
- **Structure:** Encased in Graphite Mist (`#252B28`) with an ambient 1px Deep Moss boundary (`#39483E`). Internal padding set strictly to `space-lg` (24px).
- **Header:** Contains a top metadata ribbon in monospaced annotation (e.g., `ARCH: TRANSFORMER // ACC: 98.4%`) separated by a horizontal hairline divider.
- **Interaction:** On hover, the border illuminates to Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0`), and the container transitions subtly upward by 2px with zero spring or bouncy easing.

### Form Inputs & Terminal Fields
- **Input Fields:** Obsidian Ink background (`#171A19`), 1px Deep Moss border (`#39483E`), text in Inter (`#F0EEE6`). Placeholder text in Ash Grey (`#8F9790`) at 60% opacity.
- **Focus State:** 1px Sage Signal outline (`#A8B8A0`) with zero exterior focus ring glow. Prefix labels rendered in monospaced bracket format (e.g., `[QUERY]:`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- **Checkboxes:** 14px square boxes, 1px Deep Moss border (`#39483E`). Checked state fills with Sage Signal (`#A8B8A0`) and displays a 1px Obsidian Ink interior cross or square tick.
- **Segmented Controls:** Enclosed in a recessed Obsidian Ink tray (`#171A19`) with a 1px border. Active tabs illuminate in Graphite Mist (`#252B28`) with Porcelain text.

### Motion & Kinematics
- Transitions use linear or sharp cubic-bezier curves (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Durations stay within 120ms to 240ms, providing instantaneous, mechanical feedback reminiscent of high-performance technical software.
